US tax withheld on dividends
The US keeps 15% of dividends from US-listed funds held in a TFSA or FHSA, and it can't be recovered. RRSPs are exempt under the tax treaty. Questrade reports dividends after the tax, so these amounts are estimated from what was paid.

        How these numbers are made
        
    
          
  • Values today come from Questrade's balances. The value line is rebuilt week by week from your activity history and daily closing prices.
    • 
          
  • US dollar amounts are converted at the Bank of Canada rate on each day.
    • 
          
  • Annualized return is money-weighted: it accounts for when each dollar went in.
    • 
          
  • Everything is calculated on this computer from your Questrade data. This is a summary of your records, not tax or financial advice.
